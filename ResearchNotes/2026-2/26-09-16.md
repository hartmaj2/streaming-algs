## compactor capacities

The compactor capacities decrease exponentially. The biggest compactor is the topmost one with capacity $k$.

If resulting capacity of a compactor would be $< 2$, we instead clamp it to $2$. We also notice that all these capacitors of size two just essentialy preform random sampling of every $2^{H''}$ elements where $H''$ is the topmost level which still has only capacity $2$.
